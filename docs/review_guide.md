# Swetha’s research review guide

Open the normal Illoura API address supplied by the owner, with **/review** at the
end. Use a current laptop or phone browser. Paste your personal **review key** and
choose **Open review queue**. Ask the owner for a review key if access is refused.
Do not share it: every submitted change is attributed to its key’s name.

**Buyer identities:** check the published buyer name and ABN against the linked
official register evidence. Conflicts and changed identities appear first. Select
the government body supported by that evidence, then choose **Approve**. Choose
**Reject** when the proposed identity is wrong. A rejection does not create a
replacement identity. The evidence link is filled in where available; check it
and replace it with the exact HTTPS source you used if necessary. Add a short
note explaining your reasoning. **Skip** leaves the item unchanged and hides it
for this visit. If sources disagree or the page says the register evidence changed,
skip and ask the research owner to investigate or refresh the proposals.

**Panels:** open the panel source. Check its owner, notice expiry and linked spend.
Choose whether it is relevant and record Illoura’s actual status: member, applied,
not applied, not eligible or unknown. Explain your marking briefly and choose
**Queue change**. Unknown is a valid answer. A marking does not verify eligibility,
supplier intake or panel access. Items labelled “Needs a manual panel check” still
need those checks; ask the research owner to record document-backed verification.

**Illoura profile:** enter each verified business fact, its HTTPS evidence link,
and a short note, then choose **Queue change**. Leave uncertain facts for the owner.
Do not enter personal contact details, employee lists or business-register people.

**What happens next:** a submission is **queued**, then checked against the current
research record. **Applied** means recorded with who changed it, when and the
evidence used. The published queue catches up with the next research snapshot.
**Rejected** explains why a change could not be accepted. If evidence changed,
refresh the queue, reread the sources and make a new decision. If the reason mentions
an interruption or uncertain commit, ask the owner to check the audit history
before submitting again: part of the operation may already have completed.
Use **Refresh queue and progress** to check. Do not repeatedly submit a queued item.
Processing pauses during the Sydney morning brief window or while research refresh
is busy; queued decisions wait safely.

Use **Forget key** when finished on a shared device, then close the tab. The key is
kept only in this tab’s browser session. Never paste it into a note or evidence link.
This page is for internal research only: it does not contact buyers or send email.
